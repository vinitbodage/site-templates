Hotel Information At The Wyndham Grand Clearwater Beach

Get to know the Wyndham Grand Clearwater Beach hotel before your arrival, making planning for your Clearwater visit absolutely simple. Our hotel policies are clearly outlined below and make it easy to know before you go.

Book Now

Resort Fee

$35 Resort Fee Includes:

  • 2 Beach Chairs and 1 Umbrella on the beach
  • Signature Welcome Beverage
  • In-room boxed water and refill stations on each guest floor
  • Children 5 years old & under eat free per paying adult for breakfast in Ocean Hai
  • 10% off retail in Pallavi Luxury Spa
  • Onsite dining discount passport
  • Complimentary coffee in the lobby (3am-6am, daily)
  • Pool towels for resort area use
  • Pool Pampering, Activities & Entertainment
  • High-speed WI-FI in guest rooms
  • Unlimited Local and toll-free calls
  • In-room Keurig coffee maker with premium coffees and teas
  • Access to 24-hour state of the art Fitness Center

Parking

Valet Only. Daily rate of $40.00 plus tax per day. Vehicles exceeding 6'11" that cannot fit in our garage will incur an additional daily oversized vehicle fee for each additional space used in our overflow lot.

Accepted Currency

USD

Check-In & Check-Out

  • Check-In Time: 4:00 PM
  • Check-Out Time: 11:00 AM
  • Minimum Check-In Age: 21

Pet Policy

Service animals only.

Children's Policy

Kids 17 and under stay free with adult on existing room bedding.

Deposit

First night's stay will be charged to your card 3 days prior to arrival.

Internet Access

Free Wi-Fi is available in guest rooms.

Guarantee

Credit card guarantee required on all reservations.

Rollaways

Complimentary, upon request and based on availability.

Guest Room Reservation Limit

Hotel policy allows only up to three (3) rooms reserved under one credit card guarantee and same name.

Group Policy

Ten (10) or more rooms booked by the same individual or company are considered a Group and need be booked through our Group Sales Department.

Smoking and Vaping Policy

We strictly enforce a no-smoking or vaping policy throughout all designated hotel guest rooms and indoor public spaces. A cleaning fee of $250.00 will be charged if evidence of smoking or vaping is found in a guest room.

Airport transportation

We do not offer a hotel shuttle; however, there are several convenient transportation options from both Tampa International Airport (TPA) and St. Pete-Clearwater International Airport (PIE) including: The Grouper Shuttle, Rideshare Services (Uber & Lyft), and Private Car Services (we recommend Jaunt Services Inc.).

Cancellation Policy

A reservation must be cancelled at least 72 hours prior to arrival to avoid a one night room and tax penalty. If a cancellation is not received and the reservation no-shows, a one night room and tax penalty will also apply.

Early Departure Fee

If a guest checks out of their room earlier than the originally scheduled departure date it may affect the rate and/or incur an early departure fee. Please speak with the Front Desk directly, if you unexpectedly need to shorten a planned length of stay. Front Desk staff can confirm if an Early Departure Fee or rate adjustment applies.
